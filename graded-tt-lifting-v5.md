# Graded TT-lifting and adequacy relations

## Status

**Adopted relation architecture; conditional paper derivation.**  This page
separates the two relation constructions previously both called `liftRel`:

1. a **structural free lifting**, generated by return, base and free nodes;
2. an **observational graded TT-lifting**, generated from a chosen observation
   pole by quantifying over continuations.

Only the first satisfies the strict graph equality.  The second is generally a
larger observational closure and is the relation used for the fundamental
lemma and adequacy transport.

## 1. Base data and observation pole

Let $S$ and $T$ be two graded models over the same ordered effect monoid
$(E,1,\cdot,\leq)$.  Fix answer objects $R_S,R_T$ and, for every grade $g$, a
relation

$$
\mathcal O_g\subseteq S_gR_S\times T_gR_T.
$$

$\mathcal O$ is the **pole**: it states which complete effectful observations
count as related.  It must be stable under subeffecting,

$$
g\leq g'land m\mathrel{\mathcal O_g}n
\Longrightarrow
\tau^S_{g,g'}m\mathrel{\mathcal O_{g'}}\tau^T_{g,g'}n,
\tag{Pole-Weak}
$$

and under the declared response structure $\mathcal K$.  Examples are equality
of Writer logs and values, equality of final State observations, equality of
Exception outcomes, and equality of subdistributions.

## 2. Graded orthogonality

For a value relation $V\subseteq X\times Y$ and a continuation grade $f$,
define

$$
V^{\top_f}
=
\left\{
(k,\ell)\ \middle|\
\begin{array}{l}
k:X\to S_fR_S,\quad \ell:Y\to T_fR_T,\\
\forall xVy.\ k(x)\mathrel{\mathcal O_f}\ell(y)
\end{array}
\right\}.
\tag{Orthogonal}
$$

For computations $m\in S_eX$ and $n\in T_eY$, put

$$
m\mathrel{V^{\top\top}_e}n
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall f\ \forall(k,\ell)\in V^{\top_f}.\quad
m\mathbin{\gg=_S}k
\mathrel{\mathcal O_{e\cdot f}}
n\mathbin{\gg=_T}\ell.
\tag{Graded-TT}
$$

The product $e\cdot f$ is essential: it records the computation effect before
the observation continuation effect.  Weakening coherence makes the
definition invariant under a common larger upper bound.

## 3. Definition — `TTCert`

We say that $\mathsf{TTCert}(S,T,\mathcal O)$ holds when:

1. **Pole coherence.** $\mathcal O$ is stable under effect weakening and the
   response structure $\mathcal K$.
2. **Computational closure.** TT-related values are preserved by return,
   graded bind and strength.
3. **Primitive compatibility.** Every pair of related primitive parameters is
   sent to TT-related computations.
4. **Observation reflection.** Membership in the ground pole reflects the
   selected operational observation.

Conditions (2) and (3) have the characteristic forms

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{unitTT}:&\quad xVy\Rightarrow
\eta^Sx\mathrel{V^{\top\top}_1}\eta^Ty,\\
\mathsf{bindTT}:&\quad
m\mathrel{V^{\top\top}_e}n
\land\forall xVy.\ kx\mathrel{W^{\top\top}_f}\ell y\\
&\hspace{30mm}\Rightarrow
m\gg=k\mathrel{W^{\top\top}_{e\cdot f}}n\gg=\ell,\\
\mathsf{primitiveTT}:&\quad
p\,V_{P_\beta}\,q\Rightarrow
\beta^S(p)\mathrel{(V_{R_\beta}^{\top\top})_{|\beta|}}\beta^T(q).
\end{aligned}
$$

Condition (4), `observeReflect`, connects membership in the pole back to the selected
operational observation.  Without it TT-lifting supplies semantic
compatibility, but not adequacy reflection.

:::{prf:theorem} Graded TT fundamental lemma
:label: thm-graded-tt-fundamental-v5

If `TTCert` holds and constants and algebraic primitives preserve their value
relations, every well-typed base term maps related environments to related
values or $V^{\top\top}_e$-related computations.
:::

**Proof.**  Induction on typing.  Return uses `unitTT`, sequencing uses
`bindTT`, products/functions use strength and the usual logical-relation
clauses, primitives use `primitiveTT`, and subeffecting uses `poleWeak`.
$\square$

This is the graded form of the role played by categorical TT-lifting for
Moggi's computational metalanguage.  The ordered grade appears only through
typed continuation composition and coherent weakening.

## 4. Structural free lifting

For a compatible computation relation $C_e\subseteq S_eA\times T_eB$, define

$$
\mathsf{Str}_\Delta(C)
\subseteq
\mathsf F_\Delta(S)\times\mathsf F_\Delta(T)
$$

as the least relation closed under:

- related returns;
- $C$-related base layers;
- equal free-operation tags, related parameters and pointwise-related
  continuations.

This is the former `liftRel`; the new name records that it is inductively
generated by the free carrier rather than observationally closed.

:::{prf:theorem} Structural graph law
:label: thm-structural-graph-law-tt-v5

For every compatible morphism $q:S\Rightarrow T$,

$$
\mathsf{Str}_\Delta(\operatorname{Graph}q)
=\operatorname{Graph}(\mathsf F_\Delta q).
\tag{Structural-Graph}
$$
:::

This is Chapter II's graph induction.

## 5. TT-lifting after free extension

Extend the pole to $\mathcal O^\Delta$ so that it distinguishes related
returns, base outcomes and exposed free requests, with pointwise-related
response continuations.  Applying graded TT-lifting to the extended models
gives

$$
V^{\top_\Delta\top_\Delta}_e
\subseteq
\mathsf F_\Delta(S)_eA
\times
\mathsf F_\Delta(T)_eB.
$$

:::{prf:theorem} Structural-to-observational inclusion
:label: thm-structural-tt-inclusion-v5

If the extended pole is closed under base and free constructors, then

$$
\mathsf{Str}_\Delta(V^{\top\top})
\subseteq
V^{\top_\Delta\top_\Delta}.
\tag{Str-TT}
$$
:::

**Proof.**  Induction on the structural relation derivation.  Return uses the
TT unit law, a base layer uses the base `TTCert`, and a free node uses pole
closure plus the pointwise induction hypothesis. $\square$

In general this is not equality.  TT-closure can relate computations that have
the same selected observations even when they are not generated by identical
free structure.

In particular, for a compatible observation-preserving morphism $q$,

$$
\operatorname{Graph}(\mathsf F_\Delta q)
=\mathsf{Str}_\Delta(\operatorname{Graph}q)
\subseteq
(\operatorname{Graph}q)^{\top_\Delta\top_\Delta}.
\tag{Graph-TT}
$$

The last inclusion may be strict.  The previous notes' graph equality is
therefore retained only for `Str`, not asserted for TT-lifting.

## 6. Shallow handlers

For handlers $h_S,h_T$, require

$$
p_S\,V_P\,p_T
\land k_S\mathrel{V_R^{\top}}k_T
\Longrightarrow
h_S(p_S,k_S)
\mathrel{V_C^{\top\top}}
h_T(p_T,k_T).
\tag{TT-Clause}
$$

Together with related return clauses and transparent forwarding, this is the
precise replacement for the former informal `clauseRel_h` premise.

:::{prf:theorem} TT-compatible shallow handling
:label: thm-tt-compatible-shallow-v5

If the input computations are related by the extended graded TT relation and
all clauses satisfy `TT-Clause`, their shallow-handled outputs are related by
the result TT relation.
:::

**Proof.**  Use the four structural cases: return, base layer, matching free
request and nonmatching free request.  Match uses `TT-Clause`; forwarding uses
the continuation orthogonality hypothesis. $\square$

## 7. Fundamental lemma and adequacy

The proof architecture is now:

$$
\begin{array}{c}
\text{base observation pole }\mathcal O
\\ \Downarrow\ \mathsf{gradedTTLift}\\
V^{\top\top}
\\ \Downarrow\ \mathsf{structuralLift}_\Delta\\
\mathsf{Str}_\Delta(V^{\top\top})
\subseteq V^{\top_\Delta\top_\Delta}
\\ \Downarrow\ \mathsf{TTClause}_h\\
\text{handled fundamental lemma}
\\ \Downarrow\ \mathsf{observeReflect}\\
\mathcal K\text{-structured adequacy.}
\end{array}
$$

TT-lifting does not manufacture base adequacy.  `BaseCert` supplies the
operational/denotational pole and reflection; TT-lifting propagates that
relation through types, sequencing, free operations and compatible handlers.

## 8. Recursion boundary

With fixpoints, the TT relation must additionally contain bottom and be closed
under suprema of approximation chains, or be replaced by a suitable
step-indexed/biorthogonal construction.  Chapter IV's admissibility condition
is therefore not removed.  The finite theorem on this page is the input to the
recursive logical relation, not a proof of recursive adequacy by itself.
