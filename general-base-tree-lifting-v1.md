# General base tree lifting v1

## Status

**First generalization of the Writer proof package to arbitrary algebraic base-operation signatures.**

This page identifies the Writer-independent construction and separates it from
the additional assumptions needed to compare the free base tree with a chosen
base graded monad $T$.

## 1. General base data

Assume:

1. a preordered monoid

   $$
   (B,\cdot,1,\leq_B);
   $$

2. a base signature $\Sigma_B$ with operations

   $$
   \beta:P_\beta\to R_\beta;
   $$

3. a primitive grade assignment

   $$
   |\beta|\in B;
   $$

4. free interfaces $\Delta\in\mathcal D$ with operations

   $$
   \operatorname{op}:P_{\operatorname{op}}
   \to R_{\operatorname{op}}.
   $$

No denotational base monad is needed yet. The first construction is free over
the combined signatures.

## 2. General finite interaction trees

Define well-founded trees

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{GTree}(X)::={}&
\mathsf{ret}(x)\\
&\mid\mathsf{base}_\beta(p,k)\\
&\mid\mathsf{free}_{\Gamma,\operatorname{op}}(p,k),
\end{aligned}
$$

where

$$
p:P_\alpha,
\qquad
k:R_\alpha\to\mathsf{GTree}(X)
$$

for the selected operation $\alpha$.

These are finite-depth trees; an operation result type may be infinite, so the
tree need not be finitely branching.

Tree bind is structural:

$$
\mathsf{ret}(x)\mathbin{\mathsf{bind}}f=f(x),
$$

$$
\mathsf{base}_\beta(p,k)\mathbin{\mathsf{bind}}f
=
\mathsf{base}_\beta
(p,\lambda r.k(r)\mathbin{\mathsf{bind}}f),
$$

$$
\mathsf{free}_{\Gamma,\operatorname{op}}(p,k)
\mathbin{\mathsf{bind}}f
=
\mathsf{free}_{\Gamma,\operatorname{op}}
(p,\lambda r.k(r)\mathbin{\mathsf{bind}}f).
$$

The ordinary monad laws follow by tree induction, independently of the chosen
base signature.

## 3. Ordered trace monoid

Let

$$
M=B*\mathcal D^*
$$

be the free product of the base-effect monoid and the free-interface word
monoid. A path through a tree produces an element of $M$:

- a base node $\beta$ contributes $|\beta|\in B$;
- a free node from $\Gamma$ contributes $\Gamma$;
- sequential nodes multiply in traversal order.

Adjacent base factors are multiplied inside $B$. Thus a sequence of Writer
events with grade $w$ normalizes using $w\cdot w=w$, while base/free order is
retained.

Equip $M$ with the least compatible preorder generated by:

1. the embedded base preorder $\leq_B$;
2. optional free tokens

   $$
   1\leq\Delta;
   $$

3. monotonicity under left and right multiplication.

This is proof-irrelevant at the level of endpoints. Runtime trees contain no
evidence witnessing an inequality.

## 4. Downset completion

Let

$$
Q=\mathsf{Down}(M)
$$

be the downward-closed subsets of $M$. Define

$$
1_Q=\downarrow\{1\},
$$

$$
L\otimes K
=
\downarrow\{mn\mid m\in L,n\in K\},
$$

and joins by union.

### Theorem GB-001

$(Q,\otimes,1_Q,\subseteq)$ is a unital quantale.

### Proof

The same downset-convolution proof as in the Writer instance. Associativity and
unit follow from $M$; distribution follows from setwise multiplication over
union. $\square$

Every old mixed word $E\in M$ embeds as its principal ideal

$$
J(E)=\downarrow\{E\}.
$$

Unlike the raw Writer-event presentation, multiplication has already happened
inside $B$, so Writer idempotence is respected automatically:

$$
J(w)\otimes J(w)
=
J(w\cdot w)
=
J(w).
$$

## 5. Path semantics

Define path grades:

$$
\mathsf{Tr}(\mathsf{ret}(x))=\{1\},
$$

$$
\mathsf{Tr}(\mathsf{base}_\beta(p,k))
=
\bigcup_{r\in R_\beta}
\{|\beta|m\mid m\in\mathsf{Tr}(k(r))\},
$$

$$
\mathsf{Tr}
(\mathsf{free}_{\Gamma,\operatorname{op}}(p,k))
=
\bigcup_{r\in R_{\operatorname{op}}}
\{\Gamma m\mid m\in\mathsf{Tr}(k(r))\}.
$$

The graded carrier is

$$
\mathsf{GTree}_L(X)
=
\{t\mid\mathsf{Tr}(t)\subseteq L\}.
$$

### Theorem GB-002 — General tree graded monad

Return and structural bind restrict to

$$
\mathsf{ret}:X\to\mathsf{GTree}_I(X)
$$

and

$$
\mathsf{bind}_{L,K}:
\mathsf{GTree}_L(X)
\times
(X\to\mathsf{GTree}_K(Y))
\to
\mathsf{GTree}_{L\otimes K}(Y).
$$

Subeffecting is literal inclusion.

### Proof

Tree induction. Each complete path of a bind is an input-tree path multiplied
by a continuation-tree path. Base and free nodes differ only in the element of
$M$ prepended to the induction hypothesis. Monad laws hold on underlying trees.
$\square$

## 6. Primitive nodes and source interpretation

Interpret a base request by

$$
\llbracket\beta(V)\rrbracket\rho
=
\mathsf{base}_\beta
(\llbracket V\rrbracket\rho,
 \lambda r.\mathsf{ret}(r)),
$$

which has grade $J(|\beta|)$.

Interpret a free request similarly at grade $J(\Delta)$. Return, sequencing,
functions, and branching use the tree monad and grade joins exactly as in the
Writer construction.

Therefore the value-substitution and effect-soundness proofs generalize without
change.

## 7. General first-free-head transformer

Every non-unit trace $m\in M$ has an alternating normal form. Relative to a
target interface $\Delta$, exactly one of the following holds:

1. $m=b$ is base-only;
2. $m=b\Delta e$, with $\Delta$ the first free factor;
3. $m=b\Gamma e$, with $\Gamma\neq\Delta$ the first free factor.

Let $K\in Q$ bound all exhaustive clause computations. Define
$\phi_{\Delta,K}(m)$ by:

$$
\phi_{\Delta,K}(b)=\{b\},
$$

$$
\phi_{\Delta,K}(b\Delta e)
=
\{bke\mid k\in K\},
$$

$$
\phi_{\Delta,K}(b\Gamma e)
=
\{b\Gamma e\}
\qquad(\Gamma\neq\Delta).
$$

Lift to downsets:

$$
\Phi_{\Delta,K}(L)
=
\downarrow
\bigcup_{m\in L}
\phi_{\Delta,K}(m).
$$

This is the Writer transformer with an arbitrary base prefix $b\in B$.

## 8. General shallow tree handler

For exhaustive clauses

$$
c_{\operatorname{op}}:
P_{\operatorname{op}}
\to
\mathsf{GTree}_K(R_{\operatorname{op}}),
$$

define

$$
\mathsf{handle}_\Delta(\mathsf{ret}(x))
=
\mathsf{ret}(x),
$$

$$
\mathsf{handle}_\Delta
(\mathsf{base}_\beta(p,k))
=
\mathsf{base}_\beta
(p,\lambda r.\mathsf{handle}_\Delta(k(r))),
$$

$$
\mathsf{handle}_\Delta
(\mathsf{free}_{\Delta,\operatorname{op}}(p,k))
=
c_{\operatorname{op}}(p)
\mathbin{\mathsf{bind}}
k,
$$

and

$$
\mathsf{handle}_\Delta
(\mathsf{free}_{\Gamma,\operatorname{op}}(p,k))
=
\mathsf{free}_{\Gamma,\operatorname{op}}(p,k)
\qquad(\Gamma\neq\Delta).
$$

### Theorem GB-003 — General handler grading

$$
\mathsf{handle}_{\Delta,L,K}:
\mathsf{GTree}_L(X)
\to
\mathsf{GTree}_{\Phi_{\Delta,K}(L)}(X).
$$

### Proof

Tree induction, identical in shape to `WT-002`:

- return preserves a base-only unit trace;
- a base node extends the base prefix and continues the search;
- a matching free node replaces the first $\Delta$ factor with a clause path;
- an other-interface node leaves the complete tree unchanged.

$\square$

Thus the core tree construction, trace grading, and shallow handler require no
Writer-specific property.

## 9. General operational metatheory

Assume a base machine for $\Sigma_B$ satisfying:

1. **typed response:** a request $\beta(p)$ receives only $r:R_\beta$;
2. **determinism** when deterministic adequacy is desired;
3. **termination between free heads** for the recursion-free adequacy theorem;
4. **node fidelity:** its observable base transition corresponds to the same
   $\mathsf{base}_\beta(p,-)$ tree node.

Then the Writer proof scheme generalizes.

### Theorem GB-004 — Conditional behavior-tree adequacy

Under these assumptions, the operational behavior tree of a closed computation
equals its $\mathsf{GTree}$ denotation.

### Proof

The proof of `WM-005` uses no property of log concatenation except to compress a
sequence of Writer steps into notation. Replace that compression by one
$\mathsf{base}_\beta$ node per machine request. Internal reduction soundness,
typed response, and well-founded induction give the result. $\square$

### Corollary GB-005 — General handler adequacy

The direct operational shallow handler and the structural $\mathsf{GTree}$
handler have the same behavior tree.

The four head cases are exactly the four defining handler equations.

## 10. Interpreting base-only trees in a chosen $T$

Now assume a strong $B$-graded monad $T$ with coherent subeffecting and primitive
operation denotations

$$
\beta^T:P_\beta\to T_{|\beta|}R_\beta.
$$

For a **typed base-only tree derivation**, define a fold by:

$$
\mathsf{fold}_T(\mathsf{ret}(x))=\eta(x),
$$

$$
\mathsf{fold}_T
(\mathsf{base}_\beta(p,k))
=
\beta^T(p)
\mathbin{\mathsf{bind}}
(\lambda r.\mathsf{fold}_T(k(r))).
$$

The typing derivation supplies the residual grades and final coercion into the
declared upper bound. Without it, membership of a trace downset alone need not
select a canonical factorization inside an arbitrary graded monad.

### Theorem GB-006 — Fold interpretation

The typed fold preserves return, bind, and primitive base operations. If the
subeffect semantics is coherent, it is independent of inessential rearrangement
of the typing derivation covered by that coherence.

### Proof

Induction on the typed tree/source derivation. Return and primitive cases are
definitions; sequencing is graded associativity; subeffecting is coherence of
$\tau$. $\square$

This is the general replacement for Writer's canonical isomorphism.

## 11. When is the fold an isomorphism?

Writer's base semantics is free for the unary `tell` signature modulo the
chosen Writer equations, so `run` and `quote` give an isomorphism.

For arbitrary $T$, the fold need not be:

- injective: $T$ may identify distinct operation trees by equations;
- surjective: $T$ may contain semantic elements not generated by the primitive
  operations;
- invertible: $T$ may erase the first-operation structure needed by handlers.

Therefore

$$
\mathsf{BaseTree}\cong T
$$

is an additional freeness/presentation theorem, not a general graded-monad law.

The robust general statement is the fold morphism

$$
\mathsf{fold}_T:\mathsf{BaseTree}\to T.
$$

## 12. Morphism lifting

Let $T,U$ interpret the same base signature and let

$$
q_b:T_b\Rightarrow U_b
$$

be a graded monad morphism preserving subeffecting and primitives:

$$
q_{|\beta|}\circ\beta^T=\beta^U.
$$

### Theorem GB-007

For every typed base-only tree,

$$
q\circ\mathsf{fold}_T
=
\mathsf{fold}_U.
$$

### Proof

Induction on the tree derivation. The operation case uses primitive
preservation followed by bind preservation; subeffect cases use preservation of
coercions. $\square$

This recovers the desired semantic-translation theorem without requiring
handlers to be definable directly on an opaque $T$ carrier.

## 13. Relation lifting

Let base operation parameters and results carry logical relations, and suppose
the two primitive interpretations are related. Define the free tree relation
structurally:

- returns relate according to the value relation;
- base nodes use the same operation, related parameters, and pointwise related
  continuations;
- free nodes use the same qualified operation and the analogous pointwise
  clause.

### Theorem GB-008

The structural tree relation is compatible with return and bind. Related
exhaustive handler clauses induce related shallow handlers.

### Proof

Simultaneous induction on related trees. The matching handler case uses bind
compatibility and the clause hypothesis; the base case uses the induction
hypothesis pointwise; the other-free case is immediate. $\square$

If the folds into $T$ and $U$ preserve the chosen base relation, this structural
result transports to observations in the two target models.

## 14. Writer-specific versus general

| Property | Writer proof | General status |
|---|---|---|
| tree monad laws | structural | unchanged |
| trace quantale | raw Writer traces | $\mathsf{Down}(B*\mathcal D^*)$ |
| proof-irrelevant subeffecting | inclusion | unchanged |
| shallow handler grading | $\Phi$ | unchanged |
| reduction soundness | tree equations | unchanged |
| behavior-tree adequacy | deterministic Writer machine | conditional on base-machine assumptions |
| base conservativity | canonical isomorphism | fold morphism in general |
| exact log adequacy | Writer observation | replaced by chosen base observation |
| morphism lifting | trivial Writer identity | fold naturality GB-007 |
| relation lifting | node relation | unchanged structurally |

## 15. Current general theorem candidate

The evidence now supports the following theorem architecture.

### Free extension theorem

Every preordered base effect monoid with a typed algebraic operation signature
induces:

1. a downset-quantale grade algebra;
2. a trace-graded free interaction-tree monad;
3. proof-irrelevant uppercasting;
4. exhaustive one-shot shallow handlers graded by $\Phi$;
5. structural morphism and relation lifting.

### Interpretation theorem

Every strong graded monad interpretation $T$ of the base signature receives a
typed fold from the base-only fragment. Morphisms and suitable relations commute
with that fold.

### Adequacy theorem

If the base machine is adequate for the chosen base interpretation and satisfies
the head-normalization assumptions, the free extension preserves behavior-tree
and handler adequacy.

What is deliberately no longer claimed is carrier equality
$\widehat T_b=T_b$ for arbitrary $T$. That equality is replaced by a fold, and
upgraded to an isomorphism exactly for free/presented base models such as the
Writer instance above.
